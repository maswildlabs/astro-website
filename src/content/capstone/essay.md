## The Intersection of Visual Design and Systems Operations

In professional operations and systems architecture, documentation is often mistakenly treated as a secondary requirement—an administrative task completed after structural engineering phases conclude. However, this portfolio demonstrates that the principles of document design, user experience evaluation, and structural layouts are directly linked to technical execution and organizational defensibility. 

By analyzing real-world systems frameworks through established structural design metrics, this capstone establishes that visual clarity and accessibility directly impact operational uptime, mitigate deployment risks, and preserve organizational reliability.
