## The Rationale Behind Mas Wild Labs

This rationale details the technical and style decisions I made to align my professional identity with the standards of the technology and technical writing industries. My goal was to transition my brand into an Ops Architect persona role focused on the design and validation of operational systems. The following sections explain how the Mas Wild Labs identity bridges the gap between technical complexity and user-facing documentation.

* **The Business Name:** The business name "Mas Wild Labs" is an intentional choice rooted in my identity within the roller derby community, where I have served as a Non-Skating Official (NSO) for 13 seasons. By carrying my nickname into my business name, I am leveraging my experience in officiating roller derby—a role that requires extreme precision, rule enforcement, and logic.
* **Brand Persona & Alignment:** Integrating this personal history shows high accountability and a unique, "tech-noir" perspective on problem-solving that sets my brand apart from generic corporate identities. The black/green terminal palette and circuit-pen hybrid logo were chosen to evoke the "tech-noir" precision of roller-derby rule enforcement and enterprise monitoring dashboards.
* **Title Selection:** I chose the title *Ops Architect* to reflect a career shift toward high-level workflow design. My role in piloting Kia's national kiosk program served as the catalyst for this identity shift. By engineering the primary technical frameworks and SOPs for the rollout, I redefined my professional scope from standard support to structural systems engineering.
