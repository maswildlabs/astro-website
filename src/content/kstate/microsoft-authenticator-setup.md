## Introduction
Protecting company data is everyone’s responsibility at **Mas Wild Labs**. To mitigate reputational and technical risk, we require Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all logins to the company portal from any external mobile device. MFA adds a critical layer of security, ensuring our technical findings are backed by verified access protocols.

---

## Prerequisites
Before beginning the setup, ensure you have the following:
* A smartphone or tablet with the **Microsoft Authenticator** app installed (iOS or Android).
* Your **Mas Wild Labs** company-issued laptop.
* Your current **Mas Wild Labs** login credentials.

---

## Configuration Procedure

### 1. Access Security Settings
1. Log in to the **Mas Wild Labs** company portal on your laptop.
2. Click your **Profile Icon** in the top-right corner and select **Account Settings**.
3. From the left-hand sidebar, click **Security & Login**.

### 2. Initiate MFA Setup
1. Locate the **Two-Step Verification** section and click the **Set-Up MFA** button.
2. Select **Mobile App** from the dropdown menu to ensure data integrity.

### 3. Generate and Link the QR Code
1. Click **Next** on your computer screen to display a unique **QR code**.
2. Open the **Microsoft Authenticator** app on your phone.
3. Tap the **plus (+) icon**, select **Work or School Account**, and choose **Scan QR Code**.
4. Point your phone’s camera at your computer screen to link the device.

### 4. Verify Technical Connection
1. On your computer, enter the current 6-digit code from the app into the **Verification Box** and click **Verify**.
2. This establishes a **verifiable data link** between your device and the server.

### 5. Save Backup Recovery Codes
1. Once verified, the portal will prompt you to create a **Master Password**.
2. > **Mandatory Protocol:** Store this password in a secure, secondary location; it is required to protect **Mas Wild Labs** data if your device is lost or stolen.

---

## Conclusion
Your **Mas Wild Labs** portal account is now secured with MFA. Future logins from new devices will require a **six-digit code** to maintain our industry-leading technical standards.

> **Note:** If you encounter `Invalid Code` errors, please contact the **IT Help Desk** at **extension 404** to resolve the synchronization conflict.
